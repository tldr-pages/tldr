# mkdir

> Unda saraka na uweke ruhusa zake.
> Maelezo zaidi: <https://www.gnu.org/software/coreutils/manual/html_node/mkdir-invocation.html>.

- Unda saraka maalum:

`mkdir {{njia/ya/saraka1 njia/ya/saraka2 ...}}`

- Unda saraka maalum pamoja na saraka zao mzazi ikihitajika:

`mkdir {{[-p|--parents]}} {{njia/ya/saraka1 njia/ya/saraka2 ...}}`

- Unda saraka zenye ruhusa maalum:

`mkdir {{[-m|--mode]}} {{rwxrw-r--}} {{njia/ya/saraka1 njia/ya/saraka2 ...}}`

- Unda saraka nyingi zilizo ndani ya nyingine kwa kujirudia:

`mkdir {{[-p|--parents]}} {{njia/ya/{a,b}/{x,y,z}/{h,i,j}}}`
