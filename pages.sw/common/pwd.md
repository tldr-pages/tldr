# pwd

> Onyesha jina la saraka ya sasa/inayotumika.
> Maelezo zaidi: <https://www.gnu.org/software/coreutils/manual/html_node/pwd-invocation.html>.

- Onyesha saraka ya sasa:

`pwd`

- Onyesha saraka ya sasa, na ufuate viungo vyote vya ishara (yaani onyesha njia "halisi"):

`pwd {{[-P|--physical]}}`

- Onyesha msaada:

`pwd --help`
